Catalog notes

Luminous stars only selects Gaia sources across the sky with observed absolute G ≤ −2, median distance ≤ 30 kpc and relative distance interval half-width ≤ 20%. Interstellar dust dims their measured light. Hover a star for its source ID and distance interval.

The preview adds an all-sky random sample with Gaia G between 13 and 17. Hover a preview star for its Gaia ID and Bailer-Jones distance interval. The filter applies to added stars and uses half the 16th–84th percentile range divided by the median. Sampled point density depends on survey coverage and brightness.

Named-star landmarks use HYG v4.0 coordinates adapted under CC BY-SA 4.0. The dashed line marks the approximate stellar disk. OGLE-III LMC field sources share an adopted 49.59 kpc distance and can include foreground stars. V and V−I approximate brightness and colour. OGLE-III photometric maps (Udalski et al. 2008).
